Statutory Specialty

Certificates of Compliance (CoC) for Property Sales

Under South African law (OHS Act / SANS 10142-1), you cannot transfer a residential property without a valid Certificate of Compliance. As an accredited installation electrician, Paul inspects circuits, tests earth leakage times, and issues legitimate legal CoCs recognized by conveyancing attorneys and banks.

  • Pre-transfer home sale inspections across all Pretoria
  • Straightforward rectification of genuine safety defects
  • Official registered test reports delivered promptly

Selling a House in Pretoria? Here Is How Your Electrical CoC Works
