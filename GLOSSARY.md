# Black & White Electrical (Local Electrical Services)

The domain of Black & White Electrical, an independent electrical contractor operated by Paul Badenhorst from Pretoria North, serving homeowners and businesses across Greater Pretoria with responsive on-site repairs, installations, and statutory compliance certifications.

## Language

**Approved Electrician**:
A licensed electrical contractor accredited with the statutory authority to inspect, test, and certify electrical installations.
_Avoid_: Certified contractor, handyman, technician

**Callout**:
An on-site visit to inspect, diagnose, or repair an electrical problem at a customer's premises.
_Avoid_: Dispatch, appointment, site visit

**Certificate of Compliance (CoC)**:
A statutory legal document certifying that an electrical installation complies with national safety standards (SANS 10142-1), legally required for property transfers and insurance.
_Avoid_: Safety certificate, clearance slip, compliance letter

**Distribution Board (DB)**:
The central panel containing circuit breakers that distributes electrical power across individual circuits in a building.
_Avoid_: Breaker box, fuse box, panel

**Fault Finding**:
The diagnostic process of testing circuits to locate short circuits, earth leakage, or overloads causing power failure.
_Avoid_: Troubleshooting, debugging, electrical scan

**Homeowner**:
A local resident or property owner needing electrical repairs, installations, or advice.
_Avoid_: User, lead, client

**Owner-Operator**:
A licensed tradesperson who personally handles client communications, diagnoses faults, and performs the physical electrical work without delegating to third-party call centers or subcontractors.
_Avoid_: Sole proprietor, contractor, agency

**Property Transfer Inspection**:
The comprehensive statutory testing and verification of a residential electrical installation required prior to the sale and transfer of a property.
_Avoid_: Pre-sale check, home buyer inspection

**Service Area**:
Greater Pretoria (based in Pretoria North, with mobile callouts across Pretoria East, Pretoria Moot, Centurion, and surrounding areas).
_Avoid_: Coverage, territory, zone
