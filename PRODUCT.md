## Platform

## Users

- **Primary Homeowners in Distress**: Local Pretoria residents experiencing sudden, disruptive electrical faults (earth leakage tripping, burnt geyser heating elements, burning smells, total power failure) seeking immediate contact with a trusted electrician.
- **Home Sellers & Property Conveyancers**: Sellers, estate agents, and conveyancing attorneys requiring a certified pre-transfer electrical inspection and statutory Certificate of Compliance (CoC) without manufactured defect scams or inflated pricing.
- **Homeowners Planning Upgrades**: Residents needing distribution board (DB) modernizations, surge/lightning arrestor installations, or safe backup generator/inverter transfer switches.

## Product Purpose

Provide an instant-access, high-reliability mobile web presence that connects Greater Pretoria homeowners directly to Paul Badenhorst (Approved Electrical Contractor). Success means a homeowner in an emergency or needing a CoC can identify local coverage, understand honest pricing principles, and tap directly into a phone call or pre-formatted WhatsApp chat within 5 seconds of landing on the page.

## Positioning

"Electrical Work in Black & White — Honest, Clear-Cut, No Grey Areas." Unlike franchised corporate services with call center dispatchers, unmarked sub-contractors, or predatory CoC inspection quotes, customers deal directly with Paul Badenhorst—a registered Installation Electrician with a physical Pretoria North workshop, hands-on owner-operator accountability, and transparent SANS 10142-1 statutory compliance.

## Operating Context

- **Crisis & Stress Environments**: Frequently accessed on mobile phones under poor lighting conditions, power cuts (load-shedding / tripping), or cellular network degradation.
- **Real-Time Communication**: Homeowners need direct telephone conversation or instant WhatsApp messaging; asynchronous web contact forms, user logins, and multi-step quote calculators create unnecessary friction and lead to immediate drop-off.
- **Legal Compliance Timelines**: CoC clients are bound by deed transfer deadlines and municipal escrow requirements, requiring clear guidance on turnaround times and statutory criteria.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Phone-First & WhatsApp-First**: Persistent dual mobile action dock ("Call Now" and "WhatsApp") with context-aware pre-filled WhatsApp queries.
- **Interactive Problem Triage**: Client-side interactive fault selector guiding homeowners through common emergencies (tripping breakers, dead geysers, wiring smells, CoC requirements) before dispatch.
- **Static Zero-Maintenance Architecture**: Zero-dependency static stack (Semantic HTML5, Tailwind CSS, vanilla JS) running entirely in browser; loads instantaneously with no server overhead or database dependencies.
- **Centralized Configuration**: All verified contact info, operating hours, suburbs, and statutory details live in a single config file (`site-config.js`).
