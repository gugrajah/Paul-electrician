# Trading Name and Greater Pretoria Geographic Expansion

We updated the trading name from the working title to the verified official business name: **Black & White Electrical**, operated by Paul Badenhorst. With his registered workshop base located at 15 President Steyn St, Pretoria North, the service radius was expanded from Pretoria East exclusively to Greater Pretoria (Pretoria North, Pretoria East, the Moot, and Centurion). 

The "Black & White" identity directly strengthens our authentic branding: honest, straight-talking electrical work with clear pricing and "no grey areas."
